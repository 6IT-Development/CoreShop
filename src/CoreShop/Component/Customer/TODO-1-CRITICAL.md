## C-01 — (dependent) An anonymous guest checkout can take over **any customer without a login**, including admin-created B2B customers, and gets that customer's groups, so group-only prices, payment and shipping methods apply to the guest's cart

- **Where:**
  - Component contract: `Repository/CustomerRepositoryInterface.php:30` `findGuestByEmail()`. "Guest" is not a concept in `Model/CustomerInterface.php`; it only exists implicitly as "customer whose `user` is `null`" (`CustomerBundle/Pimcore/Repository/CustomerRepository.php:47-51`: `user__id IS NULL`, and `Component/Core/{Payment,Shipping,Product}/Rule/Condition/GuestConditionChecker.php`: `null === $customer->getUser()`).
  - Customers without a user are not only guests. `OrderBundle/Controller/CustomerCreationController.php:22-85` creates customers for back-office orders and never creates a user. Admins also assign customer groups and companies to such customers in the Pimcore object editor.
  - `CoreBundle/Form/Type/GuestRegistrationType.php:90-114` (`PRE_SUBMIT`): `findGuestByEmail($data['email']['first'])`. If a match exists, it calls `$form->setData($customer)`, so the anonymous visitor's form now edits that customer.
  - `CoreBundle/Checkout/Step/CustomerCheckoutStep.php:61-70` → `CoreBundle/Customer/CustomerManager.php:40-115` `persistCustomer()`: moves the existing object into the `guest/<initial>` folder (`setParent(...)`), renames it (`setKey(...)`), re-keys and re-parents all of its addresses (`setKey(uniqid())`), saves it, and dispatches `coreshop.customer.register`.
  - `StorageListBundle/Core/EventListener/StorageListBlamerListener.php:53-73` (`onRegisterEvent`) then attaches the current cart to that customer.
  - From then on the rules read the adopted customer: `Component/Order/Cart/CartContextResolver.php:28` (`'customer' => $cart->getCustomer()`, used for product price rules inside the cart), `Component/Core/Cart/Rule/Condition/{CustomerGroups,Customers}ConditionChecker.php`, `Component/Core/Payment/Rule/Condition/{CustomerGroups,Customers}ConditionChecker.php`, `Component/Core/Shipping/Rule/Condition/{CustomerGroups,Customers}ConditionChecker.php`, all of which iterate `getCustomerGroups()` or compare `getId()`.
- **Problem:** CustomerBundle **C-02** describes the data-overwrite side for real guests. The impact is wider than that:
  - **Prices and payment:** a shop sets up a "Wholesale" customer group with cart rules ("-30 %"), product price rules, a payment provider rule ("Invoice / pay later: only for group Wholesale") and free shipping. B2B customers are created in the back office without a login. Anyone who knows (or guesses) one of their e-mail addresses (they are often printed on invoices or in e-mail footers) enters it in guest checkout and gets the wholesale prices plus "pay by invoice". The order is booked on the B2B customer's account, and the shop ships against an invoice addressed to someone else's name.
  - **Data:** the B2B customer object is silently moved from its folder into `guest/…`, renamed, and its name and salutation are overwritten with whatever the visitor typed. Its addresses get new keys and parents, and they become selectable in the visitor's address step (CustomerBundle C-02, third bullet).
  - **Customer-specific rules:** `CustomersConditionChecker` (rules for one named customer, for example a negotiated price) match as well.
- **Fix direction:**
  - Fix CustomerBundle C-02 first: never select an existing customer from a typed e-mail address in an anonymous form. Create a new guest for each guest checkout, or reuse one only if the session or cart already owns it.
  - Make "guest" explicit in this component instead of inferring it from "no user": for example `CustomerInterface::isGuest(): bool` backed by a class-definition field (or a `CustomerType` enum: `Guest`, `Registered`, `Managed`). Change `findGuestByEmail()` and the three `GuestConditionChecker`s to use it, so back-office customers are never treated as guests (see also TODO-2 **B-02**).
  - In `CustomerManager::persistCustomer()`, only set the parent and key when the customer is new (`$customer->getId() === null`). Never move or rename an existing object as a side effect of a checkout.
  - **Verify** in the storefront: create a customer through the admin order creation, put it in a group that unlocks a payment provider, then do a guest checkout with its e-mail address.
  - Add a regression test (Behat: "guest checkout with the e-mail address of a managed customer must not apply that customer's group rules").
